# DEMO SCRIPT — click path, at most 10 minutes

One business sentence per screen. Ordered by persona: executive question, technical leader
concern, business leader self-service. Dashboard draft link and app URL are in README.md.

## 1. Executive question (Daniel Osei) — ~2.5 min

**Screen: Dashboard → Executive page**
> "This is the one number the board can trust: net margin, under one fixed definition, the same
> everywhere you look."
- Point to the Margin Rate KPI (30.83%) and the "Where does profit come from?" bar chart.
- One click: Filters page → filter Category to "Electronics" → point out every widget on the
  Executive page updates together.

**Screen: Dashboard → Key Figures page**
> "And here's the same number, plus the second denominator you'll sometimes see elsewhere —
> labelled so it's never confused for a second headline rate."
- Point to the Margin Rate (fixed) counter vs. the "same dollars, different denominator" counter.

## 2. Technical leader concern (Tomas Rivera) — ~2.5 min

**Screen: Dashboard → Store operations and customer page → Trust tile**
> "This is what answers 'can I stand behind these numbers': every file this pipeline ingested,
> reconciled on this run."
- Point to Duplicates removed (3,276), Rows quarantined (1,318), Late-arrival share (1.94%).
- Point to the "Which rows were rejected and why?" table — one reason (unknown_sku), across all
  60 stores — nothing silently dropped.

## 3. Business leader self-service (Maria Santos) — ~4 min

**Screen: Dashboard → Merchandising and inventory page**
> "Six weeks before the next peak: here's where stock-outs are costing margin, and which
> promotions to cut before you repeat last year."
- Point to "What do empty shelves cost? (ESTIMATE)" — Apparel highest.
- Point to the worst-10-promotions table — 8 of 30 verdict "margin dilutive."

**Screen: App → Ask the Genie**
> "And this is how your team gets the answer without waiting on a report — type the question."
- Pick "Which promotions lost us money, even though they sold more?" from the dropdown, click
  Ask Genie, show the narrative answer and the result table together.

**Screen: App → Home**
> "One click from this working session to a page your team can come back to on their own."
- Point to the same Margin Rate, matching the dashboard exactly.
