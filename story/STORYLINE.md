Birchwood Retail Group. Personas: Daniel Osei (CDO), Tomas Rivera (VP Engineering), Maria Santos (COO).
Up to 3 observers may be present — not personas, don't address slides to them.

## Minute map

| Time | Block | What happens | Slide / artifact |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:02 | Housekeeping and persona intros | Confirm names/roles, confirm 45 min / ~25 min content, ask permission to validate what was heard | Slide 1 |
| 0:02–0:03 | Outcomes | State the three things they'll see by the end (situation confirmed, working demo, two-week pilot) | Slide 1 |

| 0:11–0:21 | Solution map + live demo | 1 minute solution map, then the demo (dashboard → Genie → app) | Slide 4, then live |

| 0:24–0:27 | Close | Next steps, ask for the decision | Slide 6 |
| 0:27–0:45 | Conversation | Objections, questions, deeper dives | OBJECTIONS.md |

## Checkpoints
- **Demo must start by 0:20.** If discovery or business value run long, cut slide 3's depth (state the row, don't read every cell) and slide 5's depth (state dates, skip the "what I need from you" line until the close) — never cut discovery, the demo, or the close.
- **Reach the close by 0:38 at the latest**, even if conversation ran long in the middle — leave at least 7 minutes for questions and the actual ask.

## Cut order if running behind
1. Slide 3 depth (summarize the table in one sentence per row instead of reading every cell)
2. Slide 5 depth (give dates only, skip the "what I need from you" line until slide 6)
3. Never cut: discovery validation (slide 2), the live demo, or the close (slide 6)
